Thin Ice Tussle

The ice is breaking up. Shove everyone else into the sea.

Bots (solo)

Move: WASD / arrows, or drag on the screen. Belly-slide: Space / Shift, or the SLIDE button: slide into someone to shove them. Shaking ice is about to drop. A golden fish makes your next slide a mega shove. Last one standing wins the round, first to 4 rounds wins.
