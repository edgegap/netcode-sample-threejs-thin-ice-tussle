Online multiplayer · 2 to 4 players · free in your browser

Thin Ice Tussle

The ice is breaking up. Shove everyone else into the sea.

How to play
  • Move WASD / arrows, or drag anywhere on a phone
  • Belly-slide Space / Shift, or SLIDE: hit someone to shove them
  • Shaking ice is about to drop. A golden fish makes your next slide a mega shove
  • Last one standing wins the round, first to 4 rounds wins

Each match runs on its own server near its players, on Edgegap. Built by an AI agent with the Edgegap MCP.
